# Third-Party Boundaries

The application source and procedural animation driver derive from the author's
own private development, with identifying names and predecessor appearance
removed. The current default appearance and toy assets are supplied by the
included procedural generator. No photograph, downloaded pet model, external
texture file or predecessor Git history is included. This is a technical
provenance finding, not an exhaustive legal originality guarantee.

Minecraft and NeoForge are dependencies, not assets included for redistribution.
Minecraft remains subject to its own terms. NeoForge and the ModDev Gradle plugin
have their respective upstream licenses. Gson and JUnit are resolved by the
dependency toolchain; no copied library source is presented as application work.
The Gradle wrapper JAR is build tooling and follows Gradle's upstream licensing.

The locally installed managed minecraft-mod-testing infrastructure is
MIT-licensed upstream tooling, verified and pinned by its installer. It is
excluded from this private source/asset backup and from the production mod.
The existing launchers still require its authorized installation; its retained
license is at `licenses/minecraft-mod-testing.txt`. Future appearance packs need
their own provenance and license review. Application code and the generator are
MIT under LICENSE; generated assets are CC BY 4.0 under LICENSE-ASSETS.md.
Paper/documentation scope is in LICENSE-PAPER.md. These author-granted licenses
do not relicense upstream dependencies or excluded personal materials.
